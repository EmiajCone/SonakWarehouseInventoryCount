Rack QR Label Generator

Loads directly from your Racks Google Sheet (the same one the app itself reads, via racksCsvUrl in config.js) — no manual copy-pasting needed. You can still hand-edit the list below before generating (e.g. to add a rack that isn't in the sheet yet, or to generate just a subset) — reloading from the sheet overwrites those edits, so generate/print first if you want to keep them. This page runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Open THIS PAGE from your live site (not a local file) before generating — the labels encode a link back to index.html on whatever domain this page is loaded from, so that scanning a printed label with a phone's own Camera app opens the counting app directly, on the right rack, instead of nothing happening or opening the wrong page.
